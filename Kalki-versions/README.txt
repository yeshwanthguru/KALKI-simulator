KALKI · கல்கி — Camera Studio (v0.10.0)
See your shot before you shoot it.

HOW TO START
  Double-click Kalki.html. It opens in your browser (Chrome or Edge recommended)
  and runs fully offline. No install, no account, no internet needed.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
  - Add camera moves from the library (18 moves: push in, orbit, dolly zoom, crane...)
  - Shape each shot: lens, shot size, subject, height, angle, speed, easing
  - Watch the camera frame and the set view (top-right) update live
  - Switch between No-code and Code (edit the scene as structured data)
  - Cinematic looks: Golden hour, Teal & orange, Night, Noir, Neutral
  - Real lens behaviour: aperture (f/1.4–f/11) controls background blur
  - Give each actor an action per shot: stand, walk, sit, talk, listen, nod, sip chai, wave, look around, shrug, invite to sit, eat a snack, talk on the phone, offer something (the last three reuse existing animations with a prop)
  - Lessons and templates (the graduation-cap button): a tour of all 18 moves, shot sizes, lenses, depth of field, dialogue coverage and camera rigs, plus ready-made shot templates
  - Camera rigs (tripod, dolly track, slider, circular track, jib, gimbal, handheld) drawn in the set view, with a low-budget tip for each
  - Phone layout: on narrow screens the view sits on top with tabs for moves and shot settings
  - Download the reference-video analysis as a PDF report (pacing chart, shots, techniques, suggestions); the shot-list PDF now shows the camera rig
  - Mark the shot size of each shot in a reference video; Kalki uses it for suggestions, the simulation and the reports
  - A sixth set, a railway platform (coach on the line, pillars and canopy, station board in English, Tamil and Hindi, benches, luggage)
  - A fifth set, a school classroom (blackboard, bench-desks, teacher's table, ceiling fan), and daylight behind the windows of the indoor sets (dark at night)
  - A fourth set, a tiled living room, and new props (scooter, bicycle, bucket, brass pots, sofa, TV, tea table with steel tumblers, wall clock, standing lamp)
  - Two new sets (village street, terrace), costumes (topi, turban, garland, dupatta, umbrella, school bag, glasses) and multi-point walk paths
  - Whole-garment costumes: kurta and pyjama, shirt and dhoti, shirt and lungi, saree and blouse, school uniform, formal shirt and trousers (they bend with the actors; plain or simply patterned cloth)
  - Teaching: assignments (a brief plus checks Kalki verifies live, a report students can hand in) and a before/after comparison of two projects
  - Import a script (Fountain or plain text) into a shot-list skeleton: scene headings, actions and dialogue become shots
  - AI reference export (frame .png + text prompts for every shot) for AI video tools
  - Document details for the PDFs: production, director, scene, shoot day, accent colour, logo and the language of the headings
  - Phone camera sensors (1/1.3 and 1/2.5 inch, with 6-10 mm lenses) and anamorphic squeeze (1.33x, 1.5x, 2x)
  - Lesson and template descriptions and the camera rig explanations are also available in Tamil and Hindi
  - Use the interface in English, Tamil (தமிழ்) or Hindi (हिन्दी); titles and notes in any script appear correctly in the PDFs
  - Dawn, noon and dusk lighting looks join golden hour, night and the rest
  - Choose who plays each role from seven avatars (the people button): all actions, placement and camera work apply to every avatar
  - Import a reference video (the film-strip button): Kalki finds its shots, names the camera moves, suggests changes to your scene and plays them as a simulation
  - Sequence actions inside a shot (for example sit, then talk, then sip chai) with the action lane
  - Click the set view to place each actor, and set where they face
  - Export a top-down blocking diagram (.pdf) of actors and camera paths
  - Frame guides (G): rule of thirds, safe areas, Reels/Shorts interface zones
  - Frame for cinema 2.39:1, YouTube 16:9, Reels/Shorts 9:16 or square
  - Pick a camera sensor (Super 35, Full frame, APS-C, Micro 4/3, 1-inch); lenses show full-frame equivalents
  - Add notes to each shot; export a printable shot list (.pdf), a storyboard sheet (.pdf), a spreadsheet (.csv), a frame (.png) or a preview video (.webm)
  - Save and open projects (.kalki.json)

LOW-END LAPTOPS
  Kalki switches to Light mode automatically if playback is slow.
  You can also toggle it with the lightning button in the top bar.

PRIVACY
  Kalki never connects to the internet and collects no data.
  Files are only saved where you choose to download them.
