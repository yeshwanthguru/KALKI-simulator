# LinkedIn post draft (not published)

**I built Kalki Simulator: see your camera movement before you shoot it.**

Most filmmakers, YouTubers and film students in India can't afford professional previs tools, so they plan shots in their head or skip planning altogether. So I built one that runs entirely offline.

**What it does today (v0.4.1):**
🎥 18 camera moves (push-in, orbit, dolly zoom, crane and more) on a timeline
🔭 Real lens behaviour: focal length, aperture and depth of field
📷 Sensor presets: Super 35, Full frame, APS-C, Micro 4/3, 1-inch
🌅 Cinematic looks: golden hour, teal and orange, night, noir
📄 One-click printable shot list and storyboard sheet (PDF), plus CSV, frames and preview video
🖥️ Runs in your browser or as a small Windows app (about 3 MB). No account, no internet, no tracking.

**Coming next:** more actors and actions (sit, talk, sip chai), Indian sets and costumes, Tamil and Hindi UI, and phone support.
