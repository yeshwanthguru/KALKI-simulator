# User interview guide (Phase 0)

Purpose: find out what filmmakers, creators and teachers actually do before a shoot, what they would pay for, and whether Kalki fits, so the next features are chosen from evidence rather than guesses. Aim for 10-15 short conversations (20-30 minutes each): about 5 YouTubers/Reels creators, 5 indie filmmakers (directors, DOPs, assistant directors) and 3-5 film-school instructors or students.

Ground rules: do not pitch; ask about past behaviour, not opinions about the future; write down exact words; ask permission before taking notes; keep notes without personal details.

## 1. Background (3 min)
- What do you make, and how often do you shoot?
- Who is on your team on a typical shoot? Who decides the camera work?
- What camera, phone, lenses and support gear do you use?

## 2. How they plan today (7 min)
- Think of your last shoot. What did you prepare for the camera work beforehand?
- How did you decide the shots? Did you write a shot list, draw, use references, or decide on the day?
- What tools did you use (paper, Excel, Canva, apps, Blender, reference videos)? What did you pay?
- What went wrong or got wasted because the camera plan was unclear?
- How do you explain a camera move to your cameraperson or crew?

## 3. Reference videos and learning (5 min)
- Do you study other people's shots? How?
- When you copy a camera move, how do you find out how it was done?
- Where do you learn camera techniques (YouTube, college, friends)?

## 4. Show the product (7 min, optional, last)
Give them the v0.7.x file or a screen share and let them try it without instructions for two minutes. Watch silently, then ask:
- What did you expect to happen? What confused you?
- Which parts would you use? Which would you ignore?
- Would you use it on a phone, a laptop, or both?
- Is the Tamil/Hindi/English interface right for you? Which language would you pick?

## 5. Money and distribution (3 min)
- Have you paid for any filmmaking or editing software? How much, and was it worth it?
- If this saved you one shoot day, what would be a fair price? Monthly, yearly or one time?
- Who in your circle would hear about a tool like this first?

## Wrap-up
- What is the one thing that would make you use it every project?
- May I contact you for a follow-up? (Record the answer only.)

## After each interview
Fill one row in a shared sheet: segment, tools used today, biggest pain, what they tried in Kalki, what blocked them, price they named, language preference, device. Review the sheet after every five interviews and note repeated themes (three or more people) separately from one-off remarks.
