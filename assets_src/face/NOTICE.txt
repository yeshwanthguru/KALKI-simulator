blazeface.json and blazeface.bin are a conversion (tools/convert-face-model.mjs) of the MediaPipe BlazeFace short-range face detector
(blaze_face_short_range.tflite, 229,746 bytes, MD5 a3dd6ec31725290770b97cec0cbf94c9) downloaded from
https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/face_detector/blaze_face_short_range/float16/latest/blaze_face_short_range.tflite
